v111 — Clean Observatory Interface

Date: 2026-06-17

Major additions:

• Reorganized Observatory screen around user understanding

• Reorganized Reflection Detail screen around insight and evidence

• Introduced insight-first presentation model

• Moved diagnostics below primary observations

• Moved evidence into expandable sections

• Reduced visual prominence of implementation details

• Preserved observation events, correlations, dynamics, and diagnostics for analyst users

• Added architectural foundation for future biometric and clinical data integration

Design Principle:

Insight First
Evidence Second
Diagnostics Third

Product Observation:

This release marks the transition from a developer-facing analysis console toward a user-facing observatory experience.

Roadmap Impact:

The application now separates:

• Session-level observations
• Evidence
• Diagnostics

This prepares the system for:

• Speaker identity tracking
• Biometric correlation
• Longitudinal pattern analysis
• Clinical and wearable integrations
